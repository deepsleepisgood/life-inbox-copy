AI processing

With AI on, the text needed for each task goes to the AI provider (NVIDIA) to file, summarise and answer; see the Privacy Policy. With it off, nothing is sent: notes are filed by simple rules on this server, Ask me quotes your notes, and there's no greeting, summaries, weekly review, image description or voice transcription.
