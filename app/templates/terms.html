Terms of Use

Version: draft 2026-11-09

Who can use Life Inbox

You must be 18 or older and provide an email address you control.

Your account

Keep your password private. You are responsible for activity under your account. Tell the operator promptly if you think your account was accessed without permission.

Your content

You own what you add to Life Inbox. You give us permission to store and process it only to provide the service to you.

Acceptable use

Do not use Life Inbox to store unlawful content, to attack the service, or to access other people's accounts.

Beta service

Life Inbox is in private beta. Features may change, and the service is provided without guarantees of availability. Keep your own copies of anything important.

Ending your account

You can stop using Life Inbox at any time. Delete my account, in Settings, erases your account and everything in it once you confirm with your password; it can't be undone, so export your notes first if you want to keep them.

AI features

With your AI switch on, Life Inbox uses an AI provider (NVIDIA) to file, summarise and answer, as the Privacy Policy describes. AI output can be wrong: it is always marked as AI, kept apart from your own words, and nothing it suggests (a topic, a fact, a date, a task) is acted on until you confirm it.
