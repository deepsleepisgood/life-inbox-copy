Privacy Policy

Version: draft 2026-10-16

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address, your name, and a one-way hash of your password. We never store your password itself.
  • Your content: notes, links, PDFs, images and voice memos you add, plus text extracted from them.
  • Security data: sign-in sessions (time created, last used, browser description) so we can keep your account secure and let you sign out.

Why we use it

Only to run Life Inbox for you: signing you in, storing and organizing your content, and keeping the service secure. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

AI processing

If AI features are enabled by the operator, the content you choose to organize may be sent to OpenAI to generate summaries, tasks and answers. A per-user setting to control this is planned.

Who can see your content

Only you, when signed in to your account. Other users cannot see your content.

Your rights

Under GDPR and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, and withdraw consent. In-app export and account deletion are planned; until then, contact the operator.

Age

Life Inbox is only for people aged 18 or older.

Contact

Grievance / privacy contact: to be published before public launch.
